Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

This explains what Zoove collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account details: your name, email address and mobile number.

Booking details: the service, time, your address (and the pin location if you share it), who the session is for, and your session history.

Optional health details: height and weight, only if you choose to share them to track progress. You can untick this at any time and the data is deleted.

Payments and rewards: package purchases, discount and referral codes. We do not store card details.

Technical details needed to run the app, such as device type and error reports.

Why we use it

To create your account, confirm it with a one-time code, book and match sessions, send you booking updates, run packages and rewards, keep the service safe, and improve it.

Who sees it

Your trainer sees your first name and full address only after the session is assigned to them. Trainers who have not taken the session see only the area and time.

Our team sees your details to run bookings and support.

We use service providers to run Zoove, such as cloud hosting and email delivery. They process data for us and may not use it for their own purposes.

We do not sell your personal data. We share it with authorities only when the law requires.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in a secured cloud database in India. Our application server runs in Singapore, so your data passes through it when you use the app. Some service providers listed below process data outside India.

We keep data only as long as needed. Sign-in codes are deleted within a day. Expired sign-in sessions are deleted after 30 days. The network address saved with your consent is removed after a year, or when you delete your account. Booking and payment records are kept as long as accounts, disputes and the law require.

Service providers

We use these providers to run Zoove: Supabase (database and file storage), Render (our server), Resend (email), MSG91 (SMS) and Sentry (crash reports, which are cleaned of names, emails and phone numbers before they are sent).

They handle data only to provide their service to us.

Your choices

You can edit your details in the app, remove optional health details, and manage your addresses.

You can delete your account in the app (cancel upcoming sessions first). Your name, phone, email, addresses and health details are then removed; we keep only the booking and payment records the law and accounting require, without your personal details.

To ask for a copy of your data or to correct it, write to admin@zoove.co.

Security

We use encrypted connections and restrict access to personal data. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in code private.

If something goes wrong

If a security incident affects your personal data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Changes, contact and complaints

If this policy changes, the app will ask you to agree again.

Questions, requests or complaints about your data: admin@zoove.co. We reply within 24 hours.
