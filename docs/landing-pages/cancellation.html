Cancellation Policy

Last updated 5 November 2026

How rescheduling and cancelling work.

Changing or cancelling a session

You can reschedule or cancel a session in the app up to 2 hours before it starts. After that, the session can no longer be changed in the app. Contact us at hello@zoove.co and we will see what we can do.

If you paid with a package credit

When you cancel in time, the credit goes back to your package, as long as the package has not expired.

If you used a discount or reward code

When you cancel in time, the code is released so you can use it again, as long as it has not expired.

If your trainer cancels or cannot come

We will tell you and look for another trainer. You keep your credit or code. You can wait for a new trainer, move the session to another time, or cancel with no loss of credit.

If we cannot find a trainer

We never cancel a session for you without telling you. If no trainer is found by the start time, we will contact you and you can wait, move or cancel.

Missed sessions

After the 2-hour window the session can no longer be cancelled in the app. If something went wrong, write to hello@zoove.co and we will look at it.

Refunds

Online payment is not live yet. Packages are activated by the Zoove team after payment is arranged with you. For any refund question, write to hello@zoove.co.
